# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (Sample)

## 1. Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Employees may start any time between 07:00 and 10:00.
Remote work is allowed up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

## 2. Leave policy
- Annual leave: 22 working days per calendar year, plus public holidays.
- Sick leave: up to 10 paid days per year; a doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive days.
- Parental leave: 18 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks fully paid for the secondary caregiver.
- Unused annual leave: up to 5 days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.

## 3. Equipment
Every employee receives a laptop on day one. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
A one-time home-office stipend of 500 EUR is available for desks, chairs and monitors.

## 4. Expenses
Submit expense claims within 30 days through the Expenses portal. Meals during business travel are
reimbursed up to 60 EUR per day. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.

## 5. IT support
Contact the IT helpdesk at helpdesk@acme.example or on the #it-help Slack channel.
Password resets are self-service at id.acme.example. Helpdesk hours are 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays.
