# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

*Fictional sample document for the RAG demo. Version 2026.1.*

## 1. Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Employees may start any time between 07:00 and 10:00.
Remote work is allowed up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements need
approval from both the manager and the People team and are reviewed every 12 months.

## 2. Leave policy
- Annual leave: 22 working days per calendar year, plus public holidays.
- Sick leave: up to 10 paid days per year; a doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive days.
- Parental leave: 18 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks fully paid for the secondary caregiver.
- Unused annual leave: up to 5 days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March.
- Bereavement leave: 5 paid days for an immediate family member, 2 days for extended family.
- Volunteering: 2 paid days per year for registered charity work.

Leave requests are made in the PeoplePortal at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3 days.

## 3. Benefits
- Health insurance covers the employee, partner and children from the first day of employment.
- The pension plan matches employee contributions up to 6% of base salary.
- Learning budget: 1,200 EUR per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over.
- Wellbeing allowance: 40 EUR per month towards gym membership, sports clubs or therapy apps.

## 4. Equipment
Every employee receives a laptop on day one. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
A one-time home-office stipend of 500 EUR is available for desks, chairs and monitors.
Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## 5. Probation and notice
The probation period is 6 months. During probation, either side may end employment with 2 weeks'
notice. After probation the notice period is 1 month for the first 2 years of service and
3 months after that.

## 6. Performance reviews
Reviews happen twice a year, in March and September. Salary changes are decided in the March cycle
and take effect on 1 April.

## 7. IT support
Contact the IT helpdesk at helpdesk@acme.example or on the #it-help Slack channel.
Password resets are self-service at id.acme.example. Helpdesk hours are 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays.
