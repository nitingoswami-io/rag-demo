# Nimbus Warranty and Returns Policy

*Fictional sample document for the RAG demo.*

## 1. Limited warranty
Nimbus routers come with a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. Refurbished units have
a 1-year warranty. Registering the product in the Nimbus app within 30 days extends the warranty of
a new unit to 3 years.

## 2. What is covered
Defects in materials and workmanship under normal use, including power adapters supplied in the box.

## 3. What is not covered
- Damage from drops, liquids, lightning or power surges.
- Units that have been opened, modified or flashed with third-party firmware.
- Cosmetic wear such as scratches.
- Loss of data or settings.

## 4. How to make a claim
Contact support through the app or at support@nimbus.example with your serial number and proof of
purchase. Support first tries remote troubleshooting. If the unit is faulty, we send a replacement
before you return the old one ("advance replacement"). The faulty unit must be returned within
14 days using the prepaid label, or the replacement is charged at the full retail price.

## 5. Returns
You can return a product bought directly from the Nimbus store within 30 days for a full refund,
for any reason. It must include all accessories. Opened products are accepted. Refunds are paid to
the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the return. Products bought
from other retailers follow that retailer's return policy.

## 6. Support hours
Phone and chat support are available from 08:00 to 20:00, Monday to Saturday. Email replies are
sent within 1 business day.
