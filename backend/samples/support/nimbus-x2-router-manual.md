# Nimbus X2 Wi-Fi Router: User Manual

*Fictional sample document for the RAG demo. Firmware 4.2.*

## 1. What's in the box
Nimbus X2 router, 12 V power adapter, 1 m Ethernet cable and a quick-start card.
The default Wi-Fi name and password are printed on the label under the router.

## 2. Status light
- Solid white: connected to the internet and working normally.
- Pulsing white: starting up. This takes up to 2 minutes.
- Solid amber: no internet connection. Check the cable from the modem to the WAN port.
- Pulsing amber: firmware update in progress. Do not unplug the router.
- Blinking blue: ready to pair with the Nimbus app or with WPS.
- Solid red: hardware fault. Contact support.

## 3. First-time setup
1. Connect the WAN port (yellow) to your modem with the Ethernet cable.
2. Plug in the power adapter and wait for the light to pulse blue.
3. Open the Nimbus app, tap "Add router" and scan the QR code on the label.
4. Choose a Wi-Fi name and a password of at least 12 characters.
Setup normally takes about 5 minutes.

## 4. Admin page
Open http://192.168.50.1 in a browser while connected to the router. The default admin user is
"admin" and the password is printed on the label. You will be asked to change it on first login.

## 5. Troubleshooting
**Slow Wi-Fi.** Place the router in a central, raised position at least 1 m away from microwaves
and cordless phones. In the app, enable "Smart Channel" so the router picks the least crowded channel.
Devices more than 2 walls away should use the 2.4 GHz band, which reaches further than 5 GHz.

**No internet (solid amber light).** Restart the modem first, wait 2 minutes, then restart the router.
If the light stays amber, check whether your provider needs PPPoE login details in Settings > WAN.

**Forgotten Wi-Fi password.** You can see and change it in the app under Settings > Wi-Fi.

**Factory reset.** With the router powered on, hold the recessed RESET button on the back for
10 seconds until the light flashes red three times. All settings are erased and the default
Wi-Fi name and password from the label are restored.

## 6. Firmware updates
Automatic updates are on by default and install between 02:00 and 04:00. You can change the
update window in Settings > System. An update takes about 4 minutes.

## 7. Specifications
Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) dual band, up to 3,000 Mbps combined. One 2.5 Gbps WAN port and three
1 Gbps LAN ports. Coverage up to 150 square metres. Power use: 9 W typical.
